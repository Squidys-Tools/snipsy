# Quick Access

After a capture, a card with its thumbnail appears in a corner of the monitor holding the cursor. This is the default after-capture action; change it in [Settings](settings.md).

## Cards

- Up to five cards stack. A sixth pushes the oldest one out to "recently closed".
- Click a card to open it in the [editor](annotating.md).
- Right-click a card to dismiss it.
- Hover a card for its actions:
  - **Copy** copies the image and removes the card.
  - **Save** saves it to the export folder and removes the card.
  - Top left: pin it ([Pinning](pinning.md)).
  - Top right: dismiss it.
  - Bottom left: open the editor.
  - Bottom right: copy the text in it ([Text recognition](text-recognition.md)). The card stays.
  - The pixel size shows at the bottom.
- Drag a card into any app to drop it as a PNG named `<prefix> YYYY-MM-DD at HH.MM.SS.png`.

Cards hide while you take another capture, so they never appear in the shot.

## Getting dismissed cards back

Dismissed cards, and cards pushed out by newer ones, are kept for the session. `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Restore Recently Closed) brings back the most recent one. Up to ten are kept. Copy, Save, Pin and Edit do not count as dismissing.

Older captures are in [history](history.md).

## Hiding

`Ctrl+Shift+O` hides or shows the whole stack.

## Options

Settings > Quick Access:

- Position: bottom left (default), bottom right, top left, top right.
- Size: Small, Medium, Large.
- Auto close after 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds (off by default). A card under the cursor is not closed.
